Mockup Studio turns a screenshot into a photograph of your product on a real device. This page gets you from nothing to an exported image in about a minute.

## 1. Pick a device

The rail on the left lists every device, grouped by kind. Click one and the camera reframes automatically — a watch and a monitor are very different sizes, so the studio composes the shot for whatever you chose.

## 2. Add your screenshot

Open the **Screen** tab and drop a PNG, JPG or WebP onto the upload area. A screen recording works too — an MP4 or WebM plays on the device and will record into an exported video.

Your file is decoded in your browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

If the screenshot does not fill the screen the way you want, change **Fit**:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cover | Fills the screen and crops the overflow. The usual choice. |
| Contain | Shrinks the image so all of it is visible, showing the screen background around it. |
| Stretch | Distorts the image to fill exactly. |

## 3. Choose a look

The **Presets** tab has a dozen premade looks, grouped by feel. *Clean studio* is a safe default; *Dark hero* is high contrast; *Transparent cutout* gives you a PNG with no background at all, ready to drop into a design.

A preset changes the scene, not your screenshot — you can switch between them freely to see which suits your app.

## 4. Frame the shot

Drag in the viewport to orbit. Right-drag or middle-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. If you lose the device, press **F** or use **Frame device** in the Camera tab.

The **Camera** tab also has nine angle presets, from a straight-on *Front* to a tight *Macro detail*.

## 5. Export

Open the **Export** tab, pick a size and press **Export PNG**.

> [!TIP]
> The **Scale** control multiplies the chosen size, and export quality does not depend on the size of your browser window — a 4K export from a laptop screen is genuinely 4K.

Turn on **Transparent** to get a PNG with no backdrop, which is what you want when the mockup is going into a design rather than being used as-is.
