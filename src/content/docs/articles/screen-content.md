The screen is where your product goes. It accepts a still image or a video, and everything is processed locally.

## Supported files

| Kind | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Images | PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, SVG |
| Video | MP4, WebM, OGG, MOV |

Files up to 250MB are accepted. Larger ones are refused rather than silently failing.

## Fitting

**Cover** fills the screen and crops the overflow — right when your screenshot came from a device of the same shape. **Contain** shows all of it and lets the screen background show around it. **Stretch** distorts to fill.

**Zoom** crops further in, and **Pan X / Pan Y** move the visible window within whatever slack the crop leaves. Panning and zooming never restart a playing video.

## Video playback

When a video is loaded, a **Playback** panel appears: play, loop, mute and speed. What plays on the screen is what gets captured when you record — see [Recording video](/docs/recording-video).

## Brand colours

Uploading a file extracts its dominant colours. Pick a target — backdrop glow, rim lights, device body, plinth — then click a colour to apply it.

This is the fastest route to a mockup that looks designed rather than generic: matching the backdrop glow to your app's accent ties the whole image together in one click.

> [!NOTE]
> Near-white, near-black and near-grey are deliberately excluded from the palette. A UI screenshot is mostly chrome, and none of it says anything about your product's identity.

## Screen background

The colour shown behind the media when it does not fill the screen, and when nothing is loaded.
