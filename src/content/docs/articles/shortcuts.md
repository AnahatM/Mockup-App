Keyboard shortcuts work anywhere in the studio, except while you are typing in a text field.

## Studio

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **F** | Frame the current device |
| **Space** | Play or pause the animation |
| **[** | Show or hide the device rail |
| **]** | Show or hide the inspector |
| **/** or **Ctrl / Cmd + K** | Search every setting, device, preset and article |
| **?** | Show the shortcut reference |

## Editing

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl / Cmd + Z** | Undo the last scene change |
| **Ctrl / Cmd + Shift + Z** | Redo |
| **Ctrl / Cmd + Y** | Redo |

Undo works a gesture at a time, not a value at a time: dragging a slider from
one end to the other is a single step back, not a hundred. It covers the scene —
the device, the lighting, the camera, the backdrop — and deliberately not your
uploaded screenshot or the layout of the panels, which are not things you mean
to undo.

## Viewport navigation

| Input | Orbit mode | Fly mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drag | Orbit | Look |
| Right-drag / middle-drag | Pan | — |
| **Space** or **Shift** + drag | Pan | — |
| Scroll | Zoom | Move forward / back |
| **W A S D** | — | Move |
| **Q** / **E** | — | Up / down |

> [!NOTE]
> **R** and **F** still work as aliases for **Q** and **E** in fly mode, for
> anyone whose fingers already learned the old binding.

> [!NOTE]
> Holding **Space** or **Shift** and dragging pans the view even without a
> middle or right mouse button — the main way to pan on a single-button
> trackpad. Space is normally play/pause: tapping it still toggles playback,
> but holding it to drag does not also toggle — the drag "wins".

> [!NOTE]
> Scrolling on a trackpad zooms (or, in fly mode, moves) at the same felt
> speed as a mouse wheel — a two-finger scroll is many small steps rather
> than a mouse wheel's few large "notches", and the studio scales by how far
> you have scrolled rather than how many events that took.

> [!NOTE]
> Plain-key shortcuts are ignored while you are typing, so naming a preset never
> triggers an action. Search and undo are the exceptions — they work from
> anywhere, because a shortcut you have to click away from a field to use is not
> worth having. While flying, **W A S D Q E R F** belong to movement rather
> than to any shortcut that happens to reuse one of those letters (**F** also
> frames the device outside of fly mode).
